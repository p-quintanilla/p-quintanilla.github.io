publication: "*Chemical Engineering Research and Design*"
abstract: "Development and laboratory implementation of a centralised economic model predictive control (E-MPC) strategy for a three-cell flotation bank, coordinating pulp level and air flow in all cells to maximise recovery while keeping concentrate grade at or above 20%. In simulation it improved recovery by up to 5% over PI control under feed disturbances, and the experiments highlight both its benefits and practical challenges such as sensor delays."
