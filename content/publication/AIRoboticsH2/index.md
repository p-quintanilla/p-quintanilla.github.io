abstract: "A systematic review of how artificial intelligence and robotics are being applied across the hydrogen lifecycle, covering production, storage and transport over the past two decades (2005–2025). It identifies where AI and robotics improve efficiency and safety, highlights gaps such as robotics for hydrogen transport and the scalability of AI models, and outlines directions for future research."
