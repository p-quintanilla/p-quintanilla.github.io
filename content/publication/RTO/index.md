title: "On dealing with measured disturbances in the modifier adaptation method for real-time optimization"
date: 2019-09-02
authors: ["Navia D", "Puen A", "paulina-quintanilla", "Briceño L", "Bergh L"]
publication: "*Computers & Chemical Engineering*"
abstract: "Extends the modifier adaptation method for real-time optimisation to include information on measured or estimated disturbances, by estimating process gradients with respect to both decision variables and disturbances. Tested on a laboratory flotation column with changing feed, including delayed disturbance information estimated with ARIMA models, the approach tracked the process optimum under continuously changing conditions."
