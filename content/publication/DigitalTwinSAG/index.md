title: "Digital twin with automatic disturbance detection for an expert-controlled SAG mill"
date: 2025-01-01
authors: ["paulina-quintanilla", "Fernández F", "Mancilla C", "Rojas M", "Navia D"]
publication: "*Minerals Engineering*"
abstract: "A digital twin of a semi-autogenous grinding (SAG) mill under expert control, combining fuzzy-logic expert control, a state-space model of the regulatory layer and a recurrent neural network of the mill. A statistical layer detects process disturbances automatically and retrains the model only when needed. Trained and validated on industrial data, it predicts mill behaviour 2.5 minutes ahead with errors below 5%."
