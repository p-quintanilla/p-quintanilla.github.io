title: "Dynamic real-time optimization to mitigate critical state effects in expert-controlled SAG mills"

authors: ["Mancilla C", "Bruna R", "paulina-quintanilla", "Navia D"]
publication: "*Control Engineering Practice*"
abstract: "A dynamic real-time optimisation layer that sits above the expert control system of a SAG mill and uses a digital twin to adjust controlled-variable limits ahead of critical states such as overload or high pressure. In simulation it reduced tonnage loss by up to 57%, shortened critical states by up to 33% and increased average throughput by up to 3.5%."
