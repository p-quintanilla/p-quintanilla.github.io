publication: "*International Journal of Hydrogen Energy*"
abstract: "Neural network and random forest surrogates of a blue hydrogen process (sorption-enhanced steam methane reforming with chemical-looping combustion) cut computation time by about 99% while keeping predictive accuracy. Combined with global sensitivity analysis, they show that the CaO-to-natural-gas ratio is the dominant driver of carbon capture and hydrogen production, and compare Sobol indices (fast) with SHAP (more interpretable but much slower)."
